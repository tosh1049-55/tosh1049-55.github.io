Research

A Study on the Construction and Properties of the Theory of Boolean Gröbner BasesHonjo Senior High School Award

Graduation thesis (in Japanese) · Waseda University Honjo Senior High School · 2026 · Advisor: Yohei Ota

I restricted the setting to polynomial rings over finite Boolean rings and rebuilt the theory of Boolean Gröbner bases from an elementary viewpoint that needs only undergraduate algebra.

A finite Boolean ring decomposes into a direct sum over its atomic elements. Using this, I introduced a "restriction" by an atomic element e: the restricted ring 𝔹e is isomorphic to the field ℤ/2ℤ, so a problem over a Boolean ring, which has zero divisors, reduces to one problem over a field for each component. With this, I proved Buchberger's criterion over Boolean rings by matching it to Buchberger's criterion over fields, without the theory of local rings used in earlier work.
